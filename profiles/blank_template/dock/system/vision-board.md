# Vision Board

> *A scratchpad for aspirations and future automation ideas*

This board captures your casual wishes, dreams, and "wouldn't it be nice if..." thoughts. The Skill Flywheel reads this board alongside your actual behavior telemetry to identify when an aspiration aligns with a pattern worth automating.

**How it works:**
- Say things like "I wish I could..." or "Someday I want to..."
- These aspirations are logged here automatically
- When the Flywheel's analytical lenses run, they look for intersections between your stated aspirations and your actual usage patterns
- If a pattern matches an aspiration, it prioritizes building a skill for it
